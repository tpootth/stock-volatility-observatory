06

Methods, verification and limitations

Methods

  • Data. Datastream total return indices (dividends included) for 24 stocks; Kenneth French daily MKT−RF, SMB, HML, RF and UMD. The French calendar defines trading days.
  • Q1. Excess simple returns on the four factors, OLS per stock-year, HAC(5) errors; α reported ×252 as %/yr; BH-FDR across 240 tests.
  • Q2. Percent log returns; GARCH(1,1) and EGARCH(1,1) with Student-t errors, one fit per stock over 2016–2025; a grid of starting values keeps the highest log-likelihood. γ < 0 = bad news raises volatility more (arch sign convention).
  • Q3. Annual unconditional SD vs annual RMS of conditional σ, both ×√252.

Verification

    Re-run with scripts/verify_against_coursework.py (arch 9.0, statsmodels 0.15) from the cleaned daily series; the build also asserts every headline figure.

    Limitations

    • One year of daily data per regression makes α noisy: 12/240 significant at 5% is what chance alone produces, and none survive FDR.
    • Volatility models are fit in-sample on the full period, so conditional σ uses parameters estimated with future data. It is not a forecast test.
    • The 2016 conditional values depend partly on the backcast starting variance.
    • The five groups are hand-built and small (4–6 stocks); hypothesis checks are descriptive, not formal tests.
    • 24 large, surviving US-listed stocks; results do not generalise to the wider market.
    • Educational coursework, not investment advice.
